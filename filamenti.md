## Starting values for the Ender-3 V3 SE

| Material | Nozzle °C | Bed °C | Fan | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLA | 200–220 | 55–65 | 100% | The easiest, ideal to start with |
| PETG | 230–250 | 70–85 | 30–50% | Tougher; tends to string, reduce speed |
| TPU (flexible) | 220–235 | 40–60 | 50–100% | OK thanks to the direct drive; print slowly (20–30 mm/s) |
| ABS / ASA | 240–260 | 95–100 | 0–20% | At the printer's limit; needs an enclosure, ventilate the fumes |

These are indicative values: the right temperature for each spool is found with a temperature tower (see `calibrazione/`).
